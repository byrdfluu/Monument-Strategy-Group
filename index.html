FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

Is the investment worth it for a business our size?

Monument anchors fees to value, not hours. As part of our diagnostic approach, we target value adds and cost savings to tailor our fees appropriately. We are in the relationship business, so we do not accept a client unless it is clear we can bring significant ROI.

How much of our team's time does this require?

Minimal. We do the heavy lifting. For the Profit Diagnostic, expect focused sessions rather than constant involvement. For those who move into an ongoing partnership, we set a working cadence that fits your schedule: a dedicated partner available when you need strategic support, not another standing meeting.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Strictly. All engagements are governed by a Master Services Agreement and Statement of Work. Your financials, strategy, and business information stay private.

What's the realistic ROI?

It varies by business, but the Profit Diagnostic alone consistently surfaces recoverable margin opportunities that dwarf the engagement fee. We quantify every opportunity with ROI projections before you commit to implementation.

Do you take over management of our team?

No. Monument Strategy Group does not manage your team. We work with your leadership to drive change. Think of us as a senior strategic partner embedded alongside you, not a new layer of management.

How do we get started?

Book a complimentary 30-minute discovery call. We listen, share whether and how we can help, and if there's a fit, you receive a proposal within 5 business days.
